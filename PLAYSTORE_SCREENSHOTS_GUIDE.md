## 🛠️ How to Capture These Screenshots Easily
Since the app is built with web technologies, you can easily capture these exact dimensions using Google Chrome:

1. Open your Live App URL in Google Chrome.
2. Press **F12** (or Right Click -> Inspect) to open Developer Tools.
3. Click the **Toggle Device Toolbar** icon (or press `Ctrl + Shift + M`).
4. In the top bar, click the dropdown (where it says "Responsive" or a device name) and select **Edit...**.
5. Add custom devices with the exact resolutions mentioned above:
   - *Mobile:* 1080 x 1920
   - *Tablet 7":* 1200 x 1920
   - *Tablet 10":* 1600 x 2560
   - *Chrome OS:* 1920 x 1080
6. Select your custom device, set zoom to "Fit to window".
7. Click the **three dots** in the top right of the device toolbar and select **Capture Screenshot**.
8. Repeat for each of the 6 screens (Menu, Gameplay, Shop, Profile, Support, Worlds).
