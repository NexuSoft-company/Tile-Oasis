Privacy Policy

Tile Oasis: Sanctuary Match | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tile Oasis: Sanctuary Match ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and data when you play our game.

2. Information We Collect

No Account or Personally Identifiable Information Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide your real name, physical address, or phone number to play Tile Oasis: Sanctuary Match.

  • Game Progress & Local Storage: Your level progress, coins, gems, collected tile themes, sanctuary unlocks, and settings preferences are stored locally on your device's internal storage.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob): The Game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may process your device's Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) and coarse IP-derived location to serve personalized or contextual advertisements and detect ad fraud.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as device model, OS version, frame rates, and crash events to maintain game stability.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle title. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take prompt corrective action.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your gameplay data:

  • In-Game Reset: You can delete all your game progress, currency, and local data at any time by opening Settings in the game and choosing "Reset Local Sanctuary Save".
  • Ad Personalization Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID on your Android device at any time by visiting Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

6. Security

We value your trust. We implement standard industry security practices, HTTPS encryption for network communication, and minimal data retention to safeguard user interactions.
